Frequently asked questions

About PALs

What is PALs?

PALs stands for Peer-Assisted Learning. It is a web application designed to help Senior High School students of STI College Cubao find tutors and study partners based on their academic needs, subjects, and availability.

Who can use PALs?

PALs is intended for Senior High School students in Grades 11 and 12 currently enrolled at STI College Cubao.

Is PALs free to use?

PALs is designed as an academic support platform for STI College Cubao students. Any fees or charges, if introduced in the future, will be clearly announced.

Do I need to download an application?

No. PALs is a website that can be accessed using an internet browser on a phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop computer.

Does PALs replace our teachers or eLMS?

No. PALs is an additional learning tool. It helps students learn from one another but does not replace teachers, classroom lessons, or the official STI eLMS.

Account and registration

How do I create an account?

Visit the PALs website and select the registration or login option. Use your official STI College Cubao eLMS school account to access the platform.

Can I use my personal Gmail or another email account?

No. PALs only accepts official STI College Cubao eLMS accounts for account validation.

Why can’t I log in to my account?

Check that you entered the correct school account and password, and make sure your internet connection is working. If you still cannot log in, contact the appropriate support personnel.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the available password recovery option through your official school account or contact the appropriate STI eLMS support personnel.

Can I use someone else’s account?

No. Each user must access PALs using their own official school account. Sharing accounts is not allowed.

Tutor and tutee matching

How does the matching system work?

PALs uses an algorithm that checks information such as subject needs, academic level, learning preferences, and availability to recommend suitable tutors or study groups.

Can I choose whether to become a tutor or tutee?

Yes. Your profile information and selected role help the system identify whether you are looking for academic assistance or willing to provide it.

What if I cannot find a tutor for my subject?

There may be no available tutor for that subject at the moment. You can try again later or check whether other students have similar subjects or topics available.

Why was I matched with someone from a different strand?

PALs considers academic needs, subject knowledge, and compatibility rather than focusing only on strands. This allows students from different strands to learn from one another when their academic needs match.

Can I change my matching preferences?

Yes. You may update your profile information, subject preferences, and availability through the available profile settings.

Does the matching system guarantee the best tutor for me?

No. The algorithm provides recommendations based on available information. The final learning experience still depends on the compatibility and cooperation of the students.

Sessions and study groups

How do I schedule a tutoring session?

After finding a suitable match, coordinate with the other student and use the available scheduling features to arrange a session.

What if my tutor does not attend the scheduled session?

You may contact your tutor through the platform to ask for an update. If the tutor does not respond or repeatedly misses sessions, you may report the situation.

Can I cancel or reschedule a session?

Yes. Inform the other participants as early as possible and arrange another schedule that works for everyone.

Can I join a study group instead of one-on-one tutoring?

Yes. PALs is designed to support both individual tutoring and group-based learning, depending on the available features and matches.

What if I do not understand the tutor’s explanation?

You can politely ask the tutor to explain the topic again or provide another example. You may also look for another suitable tutor if needed.

Chat and user safety

Can I talk about personal matters with other users?

PALs is intended for academic communication only. Avoid sharing personal matters, private details, or information unrelated to learning.

Why was my message flagged or blocked?

Your message may have contained profanity, personal information, inappropriate language, or other content that goes against the platform’s rules.

What should I do if another user sends inappropriate messages?

Do not respond with offensive messages. Report the user or conversation through the available reporting feature so the authorized team can review the situation.

Can I share pictures, links, or learning materials?

Academic-related materials may be shared if the feature is available and the content is safe, appropriate, and allowed by the platform.

Privacy and research monitoring

Does PALs collect my information?

Yes. PALs needs basic account and profile information to provide its features, such as matching, scheduling, and user management.

Does PALs monitor my activities?

The platform may record certain activities, such as login frequency, features accessed, session duration, matching attempts, and peer interactions, for system evaluation and improvement.

Is participation in the one-month monitoring phase required?

No. The one-month monitoring phase is voluntary. Students must provide separate informed consent before participating.

Will my information be shared with other students?

Your account and private information should not be shared with other users without a valid reason and proper permission. Avoid including personal information in public profiles or chats.

Can I stop participating in the research monitoring phase?

Yes. Participation in the monitoring phase is voluntary. You may communicate your decision to withdraw to the research team. Your request will be handled according to the study’s informed consent and data handling procedures.

Technical support and common problems

Why is the website loading slowly?

This may happen because of a weak internet connection, heavy website traffic, or temporary system issues. Try refreshing the page or reconnecting to the internet.

What should I do if the website suddenly stops working?

Refresh the page and try logging in again. If the problem continues, report it to the appropriate support personnel.

Why are some features not showing or working?

Some features may be unavailable because of maintenance, system errors, or temporary technical problems. Try again later.

What should I do if my profile information is incorrect?

Open your profile settings and update the incorrect information if editing is available. If you cannot change it, contact the support team.

Why can’t I send a message?

Check your internet connection and make sure your message follows the chat guidelines. Your message may also be temporarily blocked if it contains inappropriate words or personal information.

What if I experience an error while scheduling a session?

Check whether your selected schedule is valid and whether the system is working properly. Refresh the page and try again. If the problem continues, report the error.

What should I do if I encounter a bug or unexpected error?

Take note of what happened, including the page or feature you were using, and report the issue to the development or support team. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same action if it causes errors.

Can I access PALs using my phone?

Yes. PALs is designed to work through web browsers on different internet-connected devices. However, some features may appear differently depending on your screen size and browser.

Who should I contact if I need help?

You may contact the designated PALs development or support team through the contact information provided on the website.
